About Zoom

ZOOM DIGITAL AGENCY is a leading digital marketing company based in Somaliland, recognized as one of the highest-ranking and most trusted agencies in the region.

We specialize in helping businesses, institutions, and organizations grow their online presence, attract customers, and increase revenue through powerful, data-driven digital strategies. With a deep understanding of both the Somaliland market and global trends, we deliver innovative and results-oriented solutions.

Our experience extends beyond traditional marketing — we have successfully worked on election campaigns, government institutions, and large-scale business promotions, positioning us as a key driver of digital transformation in Somaliland.

Government & Health Campaign / Branding

World Breastfeeding Week 2025 — Somaliland

A full branding campaign for the World Breastfeeding Week 2025 commemoration in Somaliland, themed “Mudnaanta sii Naasnuujinta: Abuur Nidaam Taageero oo Waara” (Prioritise Breastfeeding: Create Sustainable Support Systems), held at Ambassador Hotel, 1st–7th August 2025. Delivered a complete brand identity and event materials package including a stage banner, backdrop/photo banner with red carpet setup, rollup banners (X-stand), ID cards with lanyards, branded scarfs, caps, polo shirts, posters, and a billboard design — all unified under the campaign's green-to-orange gradient identity and partner logo lineup (MOHD, ACRIF, Care, Save the Children, World Food Programme, World Vision, UNICEF, Welthungerhilfe, WHO).
